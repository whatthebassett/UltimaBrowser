The feature reel

Fast where it counts.
Yours everywhere else.

Chapter 01

A space for every part of you.

Keep Work, Personal and Play apart, each with its own tabs, its own color gradient and, if you like, its own sign-ins. Swipe between them with two fingers.

Chapter 02

Tabs, standing tall. Or lying down.

Tabs live down the side, where long titles fit. Prefer them across the top? Press Alt+S. Essentials sit up top, and tabs from the same app gather in folders on their own.

Chapter 03

Everything, one keystroke away.

Press Ctrl+K and the Control Palette finds open tabs, history, Waypoints and every command in Ultima. Or just type and search.

Chapter 04

Peek before you leap.

Alt+click a link and Glance opens it right over the page. Close it, keep it as a tab, or put it side by side in split view, up to four at once.

Chapter 05

Fewer ads. No one following you.

Ads and trackers are blocked out of the box, with the same lists as uBlock Origin. Links lose their tracking codes. One click on the shield lets a site you like through.
